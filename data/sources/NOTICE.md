# Source corpus notice

The files under `data/sources/raw/` and `data/sources/normalized/` are not covered by the repository's MIT license.

The six classical witnesses were derived from Chinese Wikisource pages identified by each record's `sourceUrl` and `pages[].sourceUrl`. Wikisource text is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>. Page links provide revision history and contributor attribution.

Changes made here: HTML navigation, scripts, maintenance notices, and obvious site chrome were removed; remaining text was split into retrieval chunks; provenance IDs and SHA-256 checksums were added. The result is an uncollated partial witness and may contain transcription or normalization errors.

The two Duan Jianye records contain bibliographic metadata only. Their books remain copyrighted and are not redistributed here.
